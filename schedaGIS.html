GeoJSON (CRS EPSG 3857 (Tile Layer) / 4326 (L.geoJSON) )

- Reference link: GISCO - GeoData - Countries (EPSG 4326), Color gradation (Colorbrewer2), Leafletjs choropleth
- Choropleth Map based on the surface area of ​​the various nation-states.
*** Note: The filters allow you to search for specific names or for areas with values ​​lower than the indicated amount; clicking on a row (in the table) highlights the country involved.



Street View Map

- Reference link: Karta View, Karta View - Photos (API)
- Map containing street-level images within a bounding box with a side length of (approximately) 500 meters..
*** Note: Perform a search within the map-centered bounding box (BB) and click on the markers to view images retrieved from the Karta View Service (please note that not all areas shown on the map may actually be covered by the Karta View Service).
